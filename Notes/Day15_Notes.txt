Day 15 Polymorphism

1)What is Polymorphism?
->Polymorphism=Many forms
In c# polymorphism means one method or object can behave in differnet ways.

There are two main types:
1)Compile time polymorphism->Method Overloading
2)Runtime polymorphism->Method overriding

2)Method Overloading
->It means same method name but differnet parameters.

3)Method overriding
->A child class provides its own implementation of a method inherited from the parent class.
